ImportantSetting up Classroom 50 (do this once, in order)
  1. Accept the GitHub organization invitation emailed to you by Classroom 50 (check your spam folder). You must do this before anything else will work.
  2. Link your NetID on Classroom 50, which connects your GitHub account to your name in the gradebook.
  3. Then open the Weekly Exercise 1 link above to accept the assignment.

If your invitation expired or never arrived, email the instructor and a new one will be sent.

Everything we did in class is documented step by step in the setup guides: installing the software, connecting RStudio to GitHub, cloning a repository into a project, and committing and pushing. If you hit an error message, look it up on the decoder page.
